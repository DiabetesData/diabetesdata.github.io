Share-safe custom series CSV policy
  • Preserves CGM and insulin values, including explicit zero-delivery rows.
  • Shifts and regenerates all structured date and time columns from datetime_local.
  • Removes raw annotation text and reconstructs approved meal and pump-event labels.
  • Normalizes trace identifiers and removes unknown columns.
  • Preserves missing rows and does not fill gaps or change five-minute measurements.
  • Reports identical rows, repeated measurements, and conflicting measurements in the source and output, with corresponding file/row numbers. Findings do not trigger automatic deduplication.
